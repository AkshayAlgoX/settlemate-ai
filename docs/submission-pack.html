1. Executive Summary

Problem: High-volume payment reconciliation across fragmented source systems (orders, payments, settlements, bank credits, refunds, chargebacks) is historically vulnerable to silent variance, duplicate overpayments, and un-grounded LLM hallucinations.

Solution: SettleMate AI establishes a strict architectural boundary: AI assists financial operations, but never controls financial truth. A deterministic 3-pass rules and invariant engine serves as the single source of truth (806.75 rec/s official benchmark, up to 1,246 rec/s scale, 98.1% accuracy). Advisory AI agents investigate isolated exceptions and emit structured claims that are mechanically verified by non-LLM validators (134,511 claims/s micro-benchmark) before double-entry ledger finalization and cryptographic decision receipt emission.

5. Honest Engineering Boundaries

SettleMate AI explicitly documents its design trade-offs and non-negotiable boundaries:

  • AI Cannot Mutate Balances: All financial ledger entries are created strictly via double-entry journal post-processing following mechanical non-LLM validation.
  • Adversarial 9/10 by Design: The 10th adversarial test injects a ₹0.47 rounding difference, which is deliberately below the ₹1.00 financial tolerance and correctly bypassed without raising false alarms.
  • Bounded Cardinality: N:M combinatorial matchings are strictly bounded to prevent NP-hard factorial explosion during high-volume spikes.
